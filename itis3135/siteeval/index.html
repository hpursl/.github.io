Website Evaluations

Apple

Apple's website is sleek, modern, and user-friendly.

  • Contrast: Excellent use of contrasting colors and typography to highlight key elements.
  • Repetition: Consistent branding and design elements across pages.
  • Alignment: Perfectly aligned content for a clean and organized look.
  • Proximity: Related elements are grouped logically, enhancing usability.

Yale School of Art

The Yale School of Art website is chaotic and overwhelming.

  • Contrast: Poor contrast makes it hard to distinguish elements.
  • Repetition: Inconsistent design elements create confusion.
  • Alignment: Misaligned content disrupts the visual flow.
  • Proximity: Elements are scattered, making navigation difficult.
